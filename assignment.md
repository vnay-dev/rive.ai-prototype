# Product Design Take-Home Task

## Overview

Large industrial organisations such as Shell manage hundreds of thousands of engineering documents, including P&IDs, engineering drawings, and other technical documentation. These documents contain tags (e.g., equipment, instrument, and pipeline identifiers) that can appear multiple times on the same page, across multiple pages within a document, and throughout hundreds of different documents.

Your challenge is to design an AI-assisted workflow that enables users to identify, review, validate, and export tag occurrences across a large engineering document repository.

## Design Challenge

Design an intuitive, scalable, and enterprise-grade Document-to-Tag Matching experience that enables users to:

- Upload engineering documents for processing.
- Review AI-extracted tag matches.
- Validate each identified tag occurrence.
- Track review progress.
- Export validated results for downstream use.

Your solution should be designed for enterprise users who work with large volumes of engineering documentation.

## Workflow

Your design should cover the following end-to-end workflow.

### 1. Review Jobs

Design a landing page where users can:

- View active and completed review jobs.
- Resume previously saved reviews.
- Search and filter jobs.
- View job status and history.
- Create a new review job.

### 2. Upload Documents

Design a document upload experience that allows users to:

- Upload one or multiple engineering documents.
- View uploaded files and basic metadata.
- Start AI extraction.

### 3. AI Extraction

Design a processing experience that:

- Displays extraction progress.
- Summarises the extracted information (e.g., documents processed, tags detected, total occurrences).
- Clearly communicates the processing status.

### 4. Review Tag Matches (Primary Design Focus)

Design the core review experience where users can:

- Browse detected tags.
- Review every occurrence across documents and pages.
- Navigate between occurrences.
- Approve or reject individual matches.
- Save progress and resume later.

### 5. Summary & Export

Design a summary screen that presents:

- Total tags identified.
- Approved and rejected matches.
- Documents containing each tag.
- Number of occurrences per document.
- Overall review progress.

Include an option to export the validated results to Microsoft Excel.

## Deliverable 1 · Product Design

Design the complete UX/UI for Step 4, Review Tag Matches, including high-fidelity screens, interactions, and reusable components. While the entire workflow should be considered, your design effort should primarily focus on delivering an exceptional review experience.

## Evaluation Criteria

Your submission will be evaluated on:

- Problem understanding
- Information architecture
- User experience and interaction design
- Visual design quality
- Enterprise scalability
- Design thinking and rationale
- Attention to detail
- Presentation and communication
